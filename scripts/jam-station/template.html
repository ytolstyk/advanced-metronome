    Multi-Instrument Gain Staging Checklist

    1. Focusrite Scarlett Preamp Gains (Ch 1 & 2): Strum/pluck your hardest. Dial the gain knob until the halo ring pulses solid GREEN. It should never flash Amber or Red (digital 0 dBFS clipping). Aim for peaks at -18 dBFS to -12 dBFS in your DAW.
    2. Alesis Nitro Max Line In 5: Set drum kit module master volume to 75% (2 o'clock). Since Scarlett Input 5 is a fixed line-level receiver, this preserves full drum dynamics without clipping the converter.
    3. Laptop Amp Sim / DAW Master Out: Keep plugin input slider at unity (0 dB). Balance track faders so the main stereo master output peaks safely between -6 dBFS and -3 dBFS.
    4. HeadRush FRFR-108 MKII Volume: Set Scarlett physical large Monitor knob to 12 o'clock (nominal line level). Power on the HeadRush and gradually raise Volume 1 from 9 o'clock to 12 o'clock to fill the room cleanly.
